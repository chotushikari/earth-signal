# Earth Signal home visual system

**Scope:** Home route (`/`). Other product surfaces retain their current implementation until they receive their own approved design pass.

## Visual world

Earth Signal is a field-instrument ledger: a calm, nocturnal observation desk where a place, a time window, and the state of evidence are more important than a dashboard full of activity. It is not a space-themed console or a collection of floating cards.

## Direction contract

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **First viewport:** A visitor sees an editorial statement of purpose beside an interactive observation trace. The trace is a navigational instrument: selecting a topic moves the active marker and changes the associated evidence state without pretending that data is live.
- **Material:** deep ink, mineral paper, oxide, water blue, vegetation green, and quiet hairlines. Surfaces are large planes; panels are bounded by rules rather than stacked shadows.
- **Type:** a high-contrast serif gives questions and locations gravity; a neutral sans carries UI; mono is reserved for coordinates, timestamps, and product identifiers.
- **Layout:** a disciplined editorial grid with an asymmetric first viewport and long horizontal rules. Dense details sit in lists and ledgers, not icon-card mosaics.
- **Signature interaction:** topic selectors update a single evidence trace and its precise status. Motion follows the line, respects reduced motion, and never obscures content.
- **Mobile:** transforms to a vertical field sheet; the trace remains legible as a short route rather than shrinking into an ornament.
- **Entry constraint:** coordinate entry only. Place-name search and drawn areas remain unavailable until an approved workflow exists.

## Cross-surface rules

- A status is always paired with plain-language meaning.
- Use one visual action color only for enabled actions; unavailable features remain restrained.
- Do not use decorative gradients, glass, generic grid overlays, ornamental orbit lines, or an icon-per-card scaffold.
- Evidence states, empty states, and limitations receive the same compositional care as successful flows.

## Direction sources and raises

The selected direction combines an observation ledger with the wayfinding discipline of a midnight transit diagram: paths are readable, turning points matter, and the active route remains legible without a geographic base map. It adopts the reference-manual challenger’s explicit state grammar: unavailable functions are visibly dormant, never disguised as calls to action.
