# Earth Signal Home visual system

**Scope:** Home route (`/`). Other routes keep their present implementation until their own design pass.

## Visual world

Earth Signal Home is a scientific intake desk: a quiet, dark reading surface where a visitor enters a location, sees the provenance of the current catalog reference, and understands which questions still need a location-aware check. It is not a marketing landing page, an orbital console, or a result dashboard.

## Direction contract

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **First viewport:** a concise evidence statement, a coordinate form, and a source panel share one working surface. The catalog label, record count, and date coverage are derived from the bundled discovery summary.
- **Material:** deep green-black ground, off-white ink, subdued mineral text, hairline dividers, and one acid-lime action signal. Panels are used only for a source record that needs visual containment.
- **Type:** neutral sans at compact, workbench scale. Rank comes from density, alignment, and weight rather than a display treatment or decorative labels.
- **Layout:** a capped editorial column divides into a workbench, a product-family strip, and a readiness register. Lists carry the information; equal feature cards do not.
- **Signature interaction:** decimal-coordinate validation is immediate and explanatory. A valid pair carries into Explore through `lat` and `lon`; invalid input names the correction instead of searching ambiguously.
- **State language:** snapshot, not a live search; needs location, not available; current product families, not analytical results.
- **Responsive:** two columns fold to a single reading order; readiness entries retain their action without relying on horizontal overflow.
- **Entry constraint:** coordinates only. Place names and drawn AOIs stay unavailable until an approved provider workflow exists.

## Cross-surface rules

- A claim must identify its source and whether it is static, live, or unavailable.
- A product listing never implies coverage, a detected event, or scientific readiness.
- Avoid gradients, glass, space decoration, mock telemetry, metric tiles, and icon-card mosaics.
- Preserve keyboard focus, error, disabled, hover, and reduced-motion states as designed states.
