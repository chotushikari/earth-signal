# Earth Signal application visual system

**Scope:** Global shell and Discover (`/`). Explore, investigation, My Earth, and Reports retain their working structure while adopting the shared shell tokens; each receives a dedicated interaction pass next.

## Visual world

Earth Signal is a light scientific research application. Warm paper ground and white information surfaces support reading and review; deep forest green identifies decisions and the only action hierarchy; dark green is reserved for maps, provenance records, and other spatial canvases. It is not a generic SaaS dashboard or a themed space console.

## Direction contract

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **First viewport:** the visitor understands the mission in one short statement, sees a properly credited mission-context image, and can begin with coordinates without searching ambiguous place names.
- **Navigation:** a compact Linear-like horizontal shell; one forest-green active state, no decorative rails. It collapses to the existing accessible drawer on narrow screens.
- **Surfaces:** one 14px radius and restrained hairline borders. Elevation is used only where a real floating control needs separation.
- **Typography:** professional sans; rank comes from scale, weight, and layout, not all-caps labels or synthetic display fonts.
- **Discovery data:** the bundled ASF catalog remains visibly labelled as a snapshot. Counts and coverage dates are derived from the normalized summary. Topic availability remains “Needs location” until a location-aware query exists.
- **Imagery:** every supplied image has a source record and credit. Optical, artist-concept, and illustrative media are context only, never represented as NISAR measurements.
- **Maps:** remain dark, spatially focused canvases inside the light application shell.

## Product rules

- Do not create named investigations, curated places, saved items, observation dates, or readiness scores without verified source data.
- Keep working Cesium, catalog, and API paths; visual work must not swap them for mock data.
- Every control has focus, hover, disabled, error, empty, and mobile states where applicable.
- Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, fake telemetry, metric tiles, decorative maps, and generic icon-card mosaics.
